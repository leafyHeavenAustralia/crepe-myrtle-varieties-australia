Quick reference

  • Pick mature size first, then climate, then flower colour.
  • Published heights vary by source. Plan around the upper end of a credible range.
  • Size tiers: dwarf 0.6–2 m, compact 2–3.5 m, medium 3.5–5 m, large 5–8 m+.
  • Pots: compact types (Acoma, Zuni, Hopi) in large containers, around 60 cm.
  • Mildew risk rises with humidity. Resistant hybrids are better, not immune.
  • Main flowering is roughly December to March, shifting with location.
  • Plants are deciduous. Winter bare branches and leaf drop are normal.
  • Hard annual pruning is not needed for flowers.
  • Genuinely blue flowers are not an established colour. Expect white, pink, red, lavender or purple.
  • One native taxon exists: Lagerstroemia engleriana (formerly L. archeriana), tropical north only.

Why sizes differ between sources: pruning, climate, soil, age, breeder potential versus garden-grown specimens, and shrub versus tree management. Established growth is roughly 30–50 cm a year in one Australian nursery’s guide, which is a rough figure only.

Planting and pruning checks

TopicRule of thumb
SiteFull sun, free-draining soil.
Planting timeNo single Australia-wide month. Winter is a practical window for deciduous plants in many southern areas. Warmer regions differ.
EstablishmentWater deeply, not often and shallow. Reduce irrigation once established, based on rain, soil and season.
PruningNot required for flowering. Use it for structure, clearance and size. Avoid repeated stub-cutting at one height and severe annual topping.
Large cultivar, small spaceReplace rather than fight it with pruning for life.
FertiliserBase it on plant needs and soil testing. Coffee grounds are not a special crepe myrtle treatment.
LifespanDecades. Extension guidance reports specimens over 50 years.
